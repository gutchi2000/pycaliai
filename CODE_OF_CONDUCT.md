# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project. Be constructive,
assume good intent, discuss evidence rather than people, and make room for
participants with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces where someone represents PyCaLiAI. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
inconsistent with this standard.

To report conduct concerns, use a private channel available through the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as
reasonably possible. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.

This policy is intentionally concise. Project decisions remain subject to the
repository license, contribution rules, and applicable platform policies.
